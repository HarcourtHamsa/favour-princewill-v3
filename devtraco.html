Introducing Devtraco

Devtraco, a property development company with 30 years of experience offers a comprehensive approach to real estate. From luxury homes to affordable housing, they serve all segments. Sustainability is key, with expertise across design, development, construction, and property management ensuring quality at every stage.

Devtraco has expanded, offering premium living (Devtraco Plus) and exceptional construction (Fabrico Builders). Landmark restaurants and Talis Property Management solidify their presence. Their Devtraco Foundation reflects a deep social commitment.

Goal

Devtraco, a leading Ghanaian property developer, approached me to revamp their website. Their current website lacked user engagement and failed to effectively showcase their diverse portfolio and commitment to quality.

Design Process

  • User Research: I conducted user research to understand the target audience and their needs when searching for property developers.
  • Information Architecture: I developed a clear and intuitive information architecture, organizing content into easily accessible sections.
  • Wireframing & Prototyping: I created low-fidelity wireframes to establish the website's layout and user flow, followed by high-fidelity prototypes for usability testing.
  • Visual Design: I designed a clean and modern user interface that reflects Devtraco's professionalism and aligns with their brand identity.

Design Solution

  • A clean and user-friendly interface with clear navigation.
  • High-quality visuals showcasing Devtraco's property portfolio.
  • Detailed descriptions of each property development project.
  • Compelling calls to action (CTAs) for contacting Devtraco.
  • A responsive design that adapts seamlessly to different devices.

The redesigned Devtraco website provides a significantly improved user experience. It effectively showcases their properties, strengthens their brand image, and is expected to generate a higher volume of leads.
